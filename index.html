Tum sabse best ho, tum bahut balanced ladki ho jo ekdum perfect h, tum creative ho, tum mehnati ho, tum pyaari ho, tum caring ho, aur sabse badi baat tum samjhti ho mujhe, aur tum mujhpar bharosa karti ho.

Khus raho aur hamesa meri raho.
